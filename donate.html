    What we can take

    • Clean clothes someone could wear tomorrow
    • Shoes in pairs, tied together
    • Coats and jackets with working zips

    What happens next

    A volunteer sorts your clothes within two days. Each tag then shows whether it went to a family, to the outlet or, if it was too worn, to textile recycling.
